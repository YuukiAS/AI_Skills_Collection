# Neutral Frontend Design Gap Review

Use the installed frontend design candidate through the normal plugin entrypoint.
Read `inputs/01-web_development_scenario.json` and write a concise review response to `outputs/response.md`.

Cover:

- what authority or source material should be inspected;
- how to handle the missing material state;
- how the interaction or transition affects closure;
- what can and cannot be claimed while the product repository remains read-only.

Do not perform network actions or modify a product repository.
