## Numerical and interpretive safeguards

Table 1 uses E2 values for the first three rows and within-scope minima/maxima of the three E4 primary own/off-site estimates for the fourth. Those ranges are seed ranges, not confidence intervals. The example interval in the report is E4 decoder seed 20260828, rounded to [0.1002, 0.1639]. Rounding is to four decimals; original precision remains in the raw inputs.

The report does not infer results for FedProx, probability ensembles, checkpoint-only performance, signed scar-fraction error, or false-negative burden merely because E1 lists them. It does not infer the missing subset definitions, assert a specific historical leakage event, or treat the M&Ms decision label as a complete quantitative replication.
